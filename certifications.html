Lagree Level 1 Certification · January & February 2027

Begin your journey to becoming a certified Lagree instructor with our comprehensive Lagree Level 1 Certification.

This immersive three-day training provides the knowledge, confidence, and hands-on experience needed to teach the Lagree Method safely and effectively.

Led by an official Lagree Master Trainer, you'll learn the fundamentals of the Lagree Method through classroom instruction, practical application, hands-on training, and coaching practice on the Mini Pro.

Apply / Register for training

What you'll learn

  • History, evolution, and science of the Lagree Method
  • The Magic 10 core principles
  • Machine safety and setup
  • Proper exercise technique and form
  • Modifications and progressions
  • Coaching and cueing techniques
  • Class sequencing and programming
  • Hands-on teaching practice with feedback

What's included

  • Three days of in-person instruction with an Official Lagree Master Trainer
  • Comprehensive training materials
  • Lifetime access to the Lagree Academy Portal
  • One private 60-minute mentorship session
  • Practice opportunities at the studio following certification
  • Opportunity to audition to join the instructor team

FAQ

Certification questions

Do I need previous Lagree or fitness-instructor experience?

No — this training is designed for all levels. You'll be guided through everything you need to build confidence as a Lagree instructor.

Is my Lagree Level 1 Certification recognized outside of the studio?

Yes. Your Lagree Level 1 Certification is globally recognized, giving you the opportunity to teach the Lagree Method around the world.

Does completing the certification guarantee me an instructor position?

No. Certification is required to teach at the studio, but does not guarantee employment. Prospective instructors will be invited to audition before joining the team.

What happens after I complete my certification?

Certified instructors can use the studio for independent practice and continue developing on the Mini Pro 2. The studio can also help create practice-teaching opportunities. When you feel ready, you can audition to join the team.

What is the cancellation / refund policy?

Certification registrations are non-refundable. If an unforeseen circumstance prevents you from attending, contact us. At our discretion, we may allow a transfer to a future date, subject to availability.
