The Lagree Method

Slow. Controlled. Intentional.

The Lagree Method is a high-intensity, low-impact workout that combines strength, muscular endurance, core stability, balance, and cardio into one efficient 45-minute class.

Every movement is performed slowly and intentionally, creating continuous time under tension to challenge your muscles while minimizing impact on your joints.

The result? A full-body workout designed to build strength, improve endurance, enhance stability, and challenge you from start to finish.

Lagree is adaptable for all fitness levels. Whether it’s your first class or your hundredth, you can progress at your own pace while continuing to challenge yourself as you get stronger.
