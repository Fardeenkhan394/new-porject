Everything you need to feel comfortable and prepared before you arrive.

  • Slow, controlled movements
  • Full-body workout
  • Instructor-led class
  • Modifications and progressions available
  • Suitable for beginners and all fitness levels

What to bring

  • Grip socks — required for class
  • Water bottle

Before you arrive

Arrive early to check in, meet your instructor, and get familiar with the Mini Pro 2 before class begins.

During class

Expect instructor cues, smooth transitions, and modifications. No previous Lagree experience is necessary.

FAQ

Common questions

I've never done Lagree. Can I still come?

Yes. Classes are designed for all levels. Instructors offer modifications so you can move at your own pace.

Is Lagree the same as Pilates?

No. Lagree is a distinct, more intense method performed on specialized equipment, with slow time-under-tension and a full-body challenge.

Is Lagree beginner-friendly?

Yes. Whether it's your first class or your hundredth, you can progress while still being challenged.

How often should I take Lagree?

For best results, many clients aim for 3–4 classes per week, with recovery in between.

Do I need grip socks?

Yes. Grip socks are required for safety and hygiene. They will be available for purchase in-studio.

What should I wear?

Form-fitting athletic wear that allows movement. Please remove hand jewelry before class.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive early enough to check in, meet your instructor, and get oriented on the Mini Pro 2.

Can I take Lagree if I'm pregnant?

Please consult your healthcare provider first. Some movements may need modification. Instructors can help with safer alternatives when appropriate.

What if I have an injury?

Tell your instructor before class. Modifications can be offered. When in doubt, seek medical advice first.

What is the cancellation policy?

Final cancellation window and fees will be confirmed with the studio and applied when online booking goes live.

How old do you have to be to attend?

Age policy will be confirmed by the studio before launch.
